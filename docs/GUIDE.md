# Kitchen guide

The five tabs are Recipes, My kitchen, Meal plan, Community and Shopping. Wide screens can use side navigation and split layouts; text size is taken into account. Supported iPhones use native Liquid Glass controls.

## Markdown

In recipe text, use `**bold**`, `*italic*` and links such as `[https://example.com](https://example.com)`. Markdown is optional: plain text works too.

## Keep track of your food

Open My kitchen and add food to the fridge, pantry or freezer. Enter the name and quantity, then the date printed on the pack. You can scan a barcode to fill available product information. Enable expiry reminders if you want them; marking food as used can be undone.

## Plan today and the week

Open Meal plan to see Today or choose a week. Add a recipe, a simple meal, leftovers or eating out. Set the day, lunch or dinner and the portions. Tap a meal to edit it, or hold and drag it to another visible day. Mark meals as eaten to keep them in the history.

From the plan, review the proposed ingredients and choose what to buy. Check quantities against your actual stock. Leftovers and eating out do not add groceries. In local mode, updating the week’s groceries keeps manual items and other weeks.

## Cook step by step

Open a recipe and start cooking mode. Follow one step at a time, show the ingredients when needed and set a timer. Timers continue when you leave the screen. Activate voice commands only if you want them; they need permission and a compatible local speech engine.

## Find ideas for everyday meals

In meal ideas, search using ingredients you have. For seasonal produce, choose an area and month, then select foods to find recipes or add them to shopping. Conversions and baking substitutions are available in the tools.

## An optional fridge assistant

The optional fridge assistant uses your configured provider and API key. Enter ingredients or analyze a photo, correct the recognized list, then request a draft. Open it in the recipe editor to check and save it. Nothing is added to your stock or shopping automatically.

## Your own illustrated cookbook

Open the cookbook tool, enter a title and choose up to 50 recipes. Confirm the selection and export the PDF. Available local and Nextcloud photos are included; private notes are not. You can share the file or print it.

## Shopping, alone or together

In Shopping, open the mode selector. Keep a local list, create or join an AvoCook shared list, or choose a Nextcloud Pantry household list. Items carry over when changing modes. For AvoCook sharing, enable notifications to hear about other participants’ additions and removals. Apple Reminders is optional on iOS.

## Your data and connections

Recipes work locally without creating an account. Connect Nextcloud Cookbook if you want to sync recipes with your own server. Planning, stock and private notes stay on the device, separately for each recipe account; they are not synced by Cookbook. Export recipes alone or make a complete kitchen backup with available photos and private data.

Community and shared lists use Firebase, including an anonymous session at startup. Web imports, product lookup and the optional assistant contact their respective services. Voice recognition uses a compatible on-device engine, without a cloud fallback.
